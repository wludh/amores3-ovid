# S 3.8–3.15 — first source-absence audit

First reader: `/root/encode_s`. Date: 2026-09-09. **Awaiting independent review.**

The eight poems 3.8–3.15 are absent from the surviving Amores section of St. Gallen, Stiftsbibliothek, Cod. Sang. 864. This conclusion rests on the primary catalogue's explicit endpoint and a direct inspection of every remaining numbered page and the rear binding material, rather than on annotation coverage or a single blank page.

## Primary catalogue and a conflicting secondary record

The [e-codices catalogue TEI](https://www.e-codices.unifr.ch/xml/tei_published/csg-0864.xml), digitized from Gustav Scherrer's *Verzeichniss der Handschriften der Stiftsbibliothek von St. Gallen* (1875), pp. 297–298, identifies the Amores section as pp. 350–396, ending at III.7.74. It explicitly records the absence of the final ten verses of poem 7 and all eight remaining elegies of Book III. The [live IIIF manifest](https://www.e-codices.unifr.ch/metadata/iiif/csg-0864/manifest.json) independently describes this section as incomplete at the end and separately identifies a Metamorphoses fragment.

A [secondary manuscript census](https://www.uhu.es/proyectovidio/pdf/descripcion.pdf), found while checking the endpoint, instead lists 1.1.1–3.9.10 on pp. 350–396. That endpoint is contradicted by both the primary catalogue and the visible end of page 396. It is not used as evidence that unannotated 3.8 or 3.9 survives. The audit does not speculate about how that secondary discrepancy arose.

The primary catalogue's page-405 fragment book number also differs from the secondary census. An exact Metamorphoses book/verse identification is unnecessary to this audit and is not asserted here: the visible fragment is separate from the Amores and is identified as such by the primary catalogue.
