## Corrections and decisions

- Verse 3 now divides the legible source words as ueni tecumque; verse 24 reads manu rather than first-pass genu.
- The source e caudata is preserved in 31 succinctę, 39 ęstus and 51 phębe. The changes are based on visible descending strokes, not a global normalization of ae.
- Verse 38 faciet is legible and no longer marked unclear; only its t is darker. Added the visible dark u in 57, a in 70 and t in the first Sīt in 80.
- Verse 73 has darker final e of reuocate, followed by an abbreviated qrites with a stroke through the q descender. Its source form is retained with an explicit schematic glyph and expansion quirites.
- Verse 53 has an underdotted o after cereri. Verse 75 has underdotted atque replaced by above-line et ne. These visible cancellations are encoded, while other uncertain relationships remain qualified.
- Added the terminal point at 20, the point between the two gis-like groups at 74 and the thin loop/connector above 42. A faint a-like trace after fac at 46 remains locally unclear.
- The three first-pass review notes at 19, 53 and 74 are now documented source uncertainty. Their ambiguity does not require the user to choose a reconstructed reading. No consequential editorial escalations remain.

Applied official TEI P5 [primary-source guidance](https://tei-c.org/release/doc/tei-p5-doc/en/html/PH.html), including the distinction between observable interventions and conjectured earlier states, and [unclear](https://tei-c.org/release/doc/tei-p5-doc/en/html/ref-unclear.html) for locally uncertain source readings. Existing project verse, header, glyph and source-description conventions remain in force.
