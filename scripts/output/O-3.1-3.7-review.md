# Witness O annotations: Amores 3.1–3.7

486 image-region annotations cover every LL line in poems 3.1–3.7. No absent printed verses were found in this range. These are bounding boxes for the printed witness, not transcriptions or scholarly apparatus claims.

Source: Internet Archive `operaovid03oviduoft`, exact canvases from `docs/data/iiif-manifests/witness-O.json`. Run `python3 scripts/annotate-o-first.py` from the repository to reproduce the annotations and the line-by-line text alignment audit.

All 486 corresponding OCR lines were checked in sequence against LL text. The reproducible audit records LL text, source OCR text, canvas, OCR line index, and normalized similarity for every match. Minimum normalized similarity is 0.763; OCR substitutions and actual edition variants explain the weaker matches. The script verifies complete unique line coverage and matching OCR/manifest dimensions, and adds 8 pixels horizontally and 7 vertically to each OCR rectangle, clamped to canvas bounds.

## Editorial ordering and visual checks

Canvas 93 (printed page 46) explicitly prints verses **47–48 before 43–46** of 3.1. This was verified directly against the page image: after verse 42, “Et tamen emerui…” and “Multa supercilio…” appear, followed by “Rustica sit…” through “Haec est blanditiis…”. The annotations use the LL content identifiers **47, 48, 43, 44, 45, 46** in that physical order. They do not infer line numbers from vertical position.

The page image also confirms 3.1.53 “Vel quotiens foribus duris inlisa pependi”, differing from the LL wording; its content-corresponding region is correctly mapped.

Canvases 103 and 104 (printed pages 56 and 57) were visually inspected to resolve the lowest OCR matches. Verse 3.7.9 prints “Osculaque inseruit cupide luctantia linguis” (LL has “cupida luctantia lingua”); this is the same verse, correctly aligned. Verse 3.7.20 starts “Nec iuvenem nec me sensit amica virum?” and the OCR's initial “Xec” and other recognition errors do not change the mapping. The 3.6 ending and 3.7 beginning on canvas 103, and 3.7.20–56 on canvas 104, match the recorded sequence.

Other regions are supported by the complete OCR/content alignment and source dimensions; this report does not claim visual inspection of every page or diplomatic transcription of the witness.

Annotation `page` values are one-based viewer page numbers (canvas index + 1). The tables and alignment audit retain zero-based canvas indices.
