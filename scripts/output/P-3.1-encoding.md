- **32 (uncertainty):** A supralinear annotation above the densum/terque area is provisionally cesaries. Its relationship to the verse and precise spelling are not asserted; it remains a manuscript note rather than an inserted reading.
- **39 (uncertainty):** A small supralinear u-like mark occurs within the contulerim area. It is retained as an uncertain manuscript annotation; no substitution or original erased state is inferred.
