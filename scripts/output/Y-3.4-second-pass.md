## Corrections and decisions

- Verse 7 reads potest. Its baseline cladas has a u above, now encoded as an addition completing claudas; the first pass included that u twice in its combined inscriptions. Mentem remains above uncanceled corpus without a reconstructed deletion.
- Verse 34 clearly has Hic metus est externae; the omitted est has been restored. Verse 30 spells precium, correcting first-pass pretium.
- The source e caudata in nequitię (10), the per suspension in semper (17), and the nasal contraction quāuis (23) are preserved. Visible et ligatures in verbal endings are now encoded explicitly, including the darker sign at 31.
- Verse 44 has dark initial t, followed by u, two underdotted and crossed letters provisionally read en, and ere. The cancellation was missing from the first pass and is now retained with local uncertainty on the canceled letters.
- The source reading ceperit (28) and pullis (42) are legible, so unnecessary unclear markup was removed. The unusual pullis/cohire and the annotation above modis remain without normalization. The small sign above esse (28) is a metamark of unknown function.
- Clearly visible terminal points omitted in the first pass are restored. The point and wavy separator at 48 remain distinct source features. No modern sentence punctuation is supplied.

The manuscript alternatives, local ambiguities and unknown functions of signs remain documented. There are no consequential editorial escalations.

Project header, verse, source-description and glyph conventions remain in force.
