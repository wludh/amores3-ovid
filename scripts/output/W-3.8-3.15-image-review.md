## Geometric limits

The UI accepts one axis-aligned rectangle per verse. Full enlarged initials necessarily make the opening-verse rectangles extend into the vertical range of verse 2. In 3.9.65, the visibly displaced `(est.` ending sits above the final word, alongside verse 64; the corrected union contains both portions of verse 65 but also some of verse 64. Verse 64 now excludes that ending. In 3.14.47, skew puts the top of the K2 signature at the same image y coordinates as the verse ending at the right; the catchword is excluded, but the rectangular union retains a small amount of signature ink. These are documented geometric limitations, not uncertain word identifications. No reading was supplied from another witness, and no consequential editorial question arose.
