Long-s was normalized to `s`; printed ampersands are encoded as `&amp;` in the numbered source text. No other witness text was consulted or used to supply readings.

The transcription preserves the witness's verse sequence and source punctuation while encoding verse lines separately. Headings and running headers are recorded in metadata, rather than silently incorporated into the verse text. Full-page line starts were inspected to record the alternating indented lines; indentation was not assigned from line-number parity alone.
