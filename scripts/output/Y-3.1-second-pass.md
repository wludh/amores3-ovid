## Corrections and decisions

- Line 12: palla, removing an unsupported i; comę preserves the source e caudata. The same letter form is retained in 27 tenerę/puellę, 50 adstrictę, 56 Ancillę.
- Line 58: final aqua, removing an unsupported m. The disturbed m-like trace in Ru…pta remains locally uncertain.
- Line 60: restored the clearly visible te after quod. Above-line es and the baseline et sign remain separately encoded because no assured cancellation establishes a replacement.
- Line 69: Mota, with the visible t crossbar, replaces first-pass Mora.
- Line 30: above-line iste is legible; its relationship to uncancelled baseline ipse remains unspecified. Line 66: the above-line loop reads o, with uncertain anchoring/role.
- Lines 23 and 45: retained literal marked forms pulsū and tū without expansions that suppress the bar or infer a nasal from expected grammar.
- Retained source indecora (34), capud (32), missam (56), utramque rogamus (61), Exiga des (66) and marginal interventions without silently normalizing them.

All original 36 notes were investigated. Supported descriptions remain observations; uncertain letter shapes, intervention functions, and unrecoverable underlying states remain documentation. The choice to preserve both visible inscriptions resolves the encoding workflow without pretending their historical relationship is known. There are no consequential editorial escalations.
