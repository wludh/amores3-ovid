## Corrections and decisions

- Verse 44 reads Faemina and ore, correcting first-pass Femina and uoce from the source letters.
- Verse 18 preserves the underdotted and crossed n in formonsa as a deletion. The already encoded cancellations at 23, 25 and 31 were inspected and retained.
- Verse 29 has baseline flexibiles and an i above the e area. The first pass incorrectly placed that i as an insertion between b and l. Its precise relationship to the baseline remains documented uncertainty.
- Verse 31 retains the canceled n, the hole, a short stroke, and sa with a raised dark s-like shape. No lost letters or definite final corrected ending are invented. The source e caudata in lęsi is retained.
- The dark final o of aeterno at 11 is legible, so unnecessary unclear markup was removed. Added the visible dark e of testes (19), t in inulta (20) and e of de (46); narrowed the dark span in subduxisset (39) to its final t.
- Added the visible terminal point after uiris in 34. The unclear group piatur and following et-like sign at 33 remain conservative source documentation, without reconstruction into an expected phrase.

The first-pass review note at 31 is now documented uncertainty. Source-image limits and unknown functions of inscriptions are preserved explicitly, with no consequential editorial escalation.

Applied official TEI P5 [primary-source guidance](https://tei-c.org/release/doc/tei-p5-doc/en/html/PH.html), including the distinction between visible interventions and conjectured earlier states, and [unclear](https://tei-c.org/release/doc/tei-p5-doc/en/html/ref-unclear.html) for locally uncertain source readings. Project header, verse, source-description and glyph conventions remain in force.
