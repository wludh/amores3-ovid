- **documented-uncertainty (geometry limitation):** these are axis-aligned rectangles over photographed, slightly sloping lines, not glyph masks. Complete enlarged initials at poem openings extend into the vertical band of the second verse, so their bounding rectangle necessarily overlaps that row. The correction uses the initial’s actual lower edge plus a small margin; it does not arbitrarily extend to the full height of the next line. Some sloping adjacent rows and the very tops of signatures at 3.3.6, 3.4.43, and 3.7.15 can touch a rectangle’s edge. Avoiding every neighboring pixel would require polygons or clipping legitimate descenders. This does not create uncertainty about verse identity.
- **escalate:** none. No consequential editorial decision was required for this image-region task.
